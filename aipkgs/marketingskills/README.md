# MarketingSkills

A box of 42 marketing skills for founders and technical marketers, covering
conversion optimization, copywriting, cold email, prospecting, SEO, AI SEO, paid
ads, ad creative, video, pricing, referrals, revenue operations, and growth.

Each skill is a self-contained `SKILL.md` (with supporting `references/` and
`evals/`) that activates when you describe the relevant marketing task.
